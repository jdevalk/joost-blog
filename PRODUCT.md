# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers who already know Joost de Valk by reputation — WordPress and SEO practitioners, open-source maintainers, founders, and people thinking about how AI is reshaping the open web. They arrive from search, social, RSS, or a direct link to one post, usually to read a single long-form essay. A growing share of "readers" are AI agents and crawlers (the site logs and even courts them via `/ask`, `/mcp`, and an NLWeb index). The job to be done: read one argument-driven piece without friction, trust the author's expertise, and leave with a clear takeaway.

## Product Purpose

A personal blog and home base for Joost's writing on SEO, WordPress, open source, AI and the web, and the open internet. It exists to publish opinionated, well-argued essays under his own name and on his own infrastructure — not a platform. Success is a piece that reads cleanly on any device, looks unmistakably like Joost's and no one else's, ranks and gets cited (by humans and machines), and never reads as generated.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, opinionated, expert. The voice is direct and confident — a craftsman with two decades of standing in his field, writing in his own register, not a content marketer. Magazine-grade typography carries the identity; the design *is* part of the product. Three words: **editorial, opinionated, crafted.**

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / dev-blog look: cream-or-white body with a purple/blue gradient accent, Inter or system-default type, identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows or `01 / 02 / 03` numbered section markers as scaffolding.
- Slop tells the detector already guards against — though note the terracotta left-rule on `<h2>/<h3>` and code blocks is *deliberate brand voice*, not a side-stripe accident.
- Anything that makes the page guessable as "AI made that": uniform entrance animations, washed-out gray-on-tint body text, bounce/elastic easing.

## Design Principles

- **The type is the brand.** Domaine Display/Text + Pitch mono carry the identity. Protect the type scale, rhythm, and line length before reaching for color or ornament.
- **Practice what you preach.** Joost writes about SEO, accessibility, performance, and the open web; the site must visibly hold those bars (semantic structure, fast, AA-contrast, agent-readable).
- **Restraint over decoration.** One deliberate accent (terracotta) against a slate/parchment field. Earn every new visual element; cards and effects are the lazy answer.
- **Readable first.** Long-form prose for a partly-ESL audience — line length, contrast, and hierarchy beat cleverness every time.
- **One voice, light and dark.** Both themes are first-class and must each feel intentional, never an inverted afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, in both light and dark themes. `prefers-reduced-motion` is already honored globally in `global.css` and every new animation must keep a reduced-motion path. Keyboard navigation and visible focus on all interactive elements. Readability is treated as an accessibility concern given the ESL share of the audience.
