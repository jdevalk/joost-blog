I talk about branding as a trust problem, not a design problem. When people buy a product, they are deciding whether they trust you, and your brand is the sum of everything that shapes that decision. I cover the accidental origins of the Yoast brand, how naming a company after yourself creates complications when you eventually sell, what made Yoast’s visual identity work at scale, and what I would do differently building Emilia Capital as a new brand from scratch. I also discuss the ROI of branding investment and when it starts to see diminishing returns.
