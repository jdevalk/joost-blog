I cover my background in SEO and how I came to build Yoast, starting from an IT career and moving into consulting for large brands. I discuss the state of SEO in 2016: what Google is doing, how keyword-based thinking is evolving, and why the plugin's "SEO for everyone" approach matters. I also talk about what Yoast had reached by then, with around 9 million sites using the plugin, and what that kind of scale means for how we build and release software.
