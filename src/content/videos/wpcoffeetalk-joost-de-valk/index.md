I talk about the transition from running Yoast to investing through Emilia Capital, and what I am building now. I cover how I founded Yoast in 2010 with Marieke and what led us to sell in 2021, what we look for in companies we invest in, and why we want to reinvest back into the WordPress ecosystem specifically. I also discuss my son speaking at WordCamp Europe at 17, why the Dutch WordPress community is larger than people expect, and what the next public project is I am hoping to release soon.
