I share why I track CMS market share and what the trends show: a few platforms doing well while most others slowly disappear. I talk about schema markup as underused infrastructure for the web, where the future of rich snippets is heading, and why a more fully schema-augmented web would benefit everyone. The conversation also covers what technical SEO actually means in 2021 now that most speed and design problems are increasingly handled by the platform itself, and what that means for where webmasters should be spending their effort.
