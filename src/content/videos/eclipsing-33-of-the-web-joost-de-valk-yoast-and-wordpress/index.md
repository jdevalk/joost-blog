I talk through the origin of the Yoast name, how the company started from SEO consulting, and how WordPress grew alongside the business. I cover what it means that WordPress has reached roughly a third of the web, the difference between WordPress.com and WordPress.org, and how smaller companies can meaningfully contribute to pushing the platform forward. I also discuss the bootstrapped model at Yoast, including why we never took outside investment and how that shaped our growth.
