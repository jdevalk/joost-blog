Richard Haeser and I present the state of the Yoast SEO for TYPO3 extension at TYPO3camp Venlo. I explain why we built a TYPO3 version of the plugin, what the analysis library does and how it integrates into TYPO3's record system, and what the roadmap looks like. We had already shipped Yoast SEO for TYPO3 and were releasing updates every two weeks at the time, covering more languages, better readability checks, and ongoing improvements to how the analysis tracks what Google actually rewards.
