SEO had already moved well beyond technical tricks by 2015, and I give a talk here about what I call holistic SEO: the idea that ranking well requires a combination of technical quality, genuinely good content, and real brand authority. I explain why the people I knew running SEO for major brands were facing the same challenges as smaller sites, and why WordPress users were actually in a better position to adapt. The central argument is that the best SEO strategy is to become the best result for your topic, and everything else follows from that.
