Marieke and I make the case that good content and good SEO are not in conflict, but that focusing too narrowly on SEO copywriting is its own trap. I explain why Google's mission, to organize all the world's information and make it accessible, tells you exactly what you need to do: create content that is genuinely useful and clear. Marieke covers the writing side in depth, and together we walk through what it looks like to produce something that ranks well and that readers actually want to read.
