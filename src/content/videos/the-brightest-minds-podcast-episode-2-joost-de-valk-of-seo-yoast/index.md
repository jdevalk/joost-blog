I share the history of how I got into SEO, starting as a consultant and blogger in 2004, and how that led to building Yoast SEO. I cover the early days of the blogging world, how conference speaking and connections to people like Dave Naylor and Danny Sullivan accelerated my visibility, and what the "aha moment" was when the plugin hit a million users. I also talk about growing SEO in 2020, what the plugin's green-light system was designed to solve for non-technical site owners, and how I think about what it means to be the best result for a given topic.
