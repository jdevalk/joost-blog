A short clip from a longer interview where I discuss the Yoast SEO traffic light system and why we built it the way we did. I explain our shift from a keyword-based approach toward key phrases, why recognizing language variations matters, and how SEO behaves differently depending on which language you are writing in. I also touch on why keyword density remains relevant in smaller language markets like Dutch even as it matters less in English.
