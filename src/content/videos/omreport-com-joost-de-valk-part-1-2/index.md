An early interview from 2011 where I describe how I got into SEO and WordPress, and what the early Yoast plugin business looked like. I explain my background: I studied theology and international business, dropped out, and went to work as a Java developer, but I had been building websites since age 12. I talk about how giving away free plugins generated consulting leads, how Yoast SEO was growing beyond a million sites at the time, and how I thought about combining a coder's instinct with marketing.
