This is my YoastCon 2019 opening talk, where I explain what "SEO for everyone" actually means to us. I walk through how SEO has evolved from a purely technical discipline toward something that spans discovery, indexing, and ranking, and why voice search had already changed how search engines work even before most people were using it. With 9 million sites running Yoast SEO at the time, I also talk about what that scale means for how we think about what should be free versus premium.
