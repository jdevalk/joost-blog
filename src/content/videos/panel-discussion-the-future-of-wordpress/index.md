A Q&A panel on the future of WordPress where I discuss the FAIR Package Manager, contributor funding, and what governance should look like for a project at WordPress's scale. I talk about why the supply chain security incidents of 2024 were a turning point, what decentralized distribution actually requires in practice, and how the community can move forward without fragmenting. The conversation covers questions from the audience and goes into where I think the ecosystem needs to go over the next few years.
