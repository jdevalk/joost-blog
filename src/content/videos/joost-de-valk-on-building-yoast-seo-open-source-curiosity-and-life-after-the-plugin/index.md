I talk through the path from growing up in a small Dutch town to building one of the most-used WordPress plugins in the world. I share how a shared high school background with several of the people who later joined Yoast shaped the company's culture, and how the Dutch WordPress community ended up being larger than you might expect. The conversation covers what I am building now with Progress Planner and what it is like to go from running a 140-person company to working on new things from scratch, and why I consider the slower pace a feature rather than a drawback.
