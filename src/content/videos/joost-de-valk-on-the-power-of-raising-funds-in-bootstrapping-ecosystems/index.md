I talk about why Yoast was bootstrapped and what I think we could have done differently if we had taken investment earlier. As an investor through Emilia Capital, I explain what we look for in WordPress ecosystem companies: not just growth potential, but whether the founder only wants to make money or actually cares about doing something meaningful. I cover how much early-stage investment makes sense in a bootstrapping ecosystem, why 200K revenue is probably too small for meaningful external capital, and what self-awareness in a founder looks like from our side of the conversation.
