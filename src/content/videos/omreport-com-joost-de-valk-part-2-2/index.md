The second part of this 2011 interview covers the consulting side of my work at the time. I discuss the difference between doing SEO for large enterprise clients versus smaller sites, how SEO in Europe lagged the US by roughly six months, and why German and Dutch SEO behaved differently from English-language search due to weaker language recognition in Google's algorithms. I also talk about day rates, the value of coming in as a senior consultant, and why pricing at the right level actually makes your advice land better.
