I talk through how Yoast SEO went from a hobby project to a company of nearly 120 people and 6.5 million installs. I describe what changed when the plugin crossed a million users and my wife made clear we needed to actually build a business around it. I cover what Yoast SEO does, what has changed in recent versions, and the SEO fundamentals that still matter regardless of how the plugin handles the technical side. This is a practical conversation for WordPress consultants who want to use the plugin more effectively for their clients.
