I cover the full arc of building Yoast: why I combined my small plugins into one, the moment my wife Marieke made it clear I needed to actually run it as a business, and what the green-light system was designed to solve. I talk about the founding story, the freemium model, the biggest product mistake we ever made, and how I think about when and how companies should invest in SEO. The second half of the conversation covers AI’s impact on content: why I think large language models will increase wealth inequality and what that means for creators who are trying to build authentic authority in their field.
